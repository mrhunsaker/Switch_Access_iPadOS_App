# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
SwitchPlay is a collaborative project intended to improve access, participation, and autonomy. We are committed to a welcoming and respectful environment regardless of disability, visual ability, age, experience, identity, background, or role.

Individuals with CVI and other disabilities must be treated as experts about their own experiences.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Listen to lived experience and make room for different communication and participation styles.
- Use inclusive, non-stigmatizing language.
- Critique ideas and implementation choices, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and practical reasoning.
- Respect privacy, consent, and communication preferences.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, correct mistakes, and credit contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Ableist, demeaning, or exclusionary language.
- Dismissing an individual's stated access needs or lived experience.
- Sharing private or identifying information without explicit permission.
- Publishing identifiable participant information, images, recordings, or medical details without appropriate consent and authorization.
- Deliberate disruption or repeated unwanted contact.

## Scope
This code applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and project-related public communications.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub's private contact or reporting options. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as practical and protect reporter privacy to the extent possible.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove a person from project spaces depending on severity and pattern. Decisions should be proportionate and documented where appropriate.
